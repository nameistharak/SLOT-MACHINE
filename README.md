# **Slot Machine Game 🎰**

A fun, interactive slot machine game built in Python! Test your luck by spinning the reels, betting on multiple lines, and winning based on symbol combinations.

# **Features**

**Dynamic Slot Machine:** Spins a 3x3 grid with randomized symbols.

**Betting System:** Players can choose the number of lines to bet on and the amount to wager.

**Winning Combinations:** Horizontal, vertical, and diagonal matches pay out based on symbol values.

**User-Friendly Interface:** Simple prompts guide the user throughout the game.

# **Rules**

**Deposit Funds:** Start by entering your initial deposit amount.

**Place Bets:**

  - Choose the number of lines to bet on (1-3).
  
  - Enter your bet amount per line.
  
**Spin the Machine:** The slot machine spins and displays the result.

**Win or Lose:**

  - Matches on horizontal, vertical, or diagonal lines yield winnings based on the symbol values.

  - Total winnings are calculated and displayed after each spin.

**End the Game:** Continue spinning until you quit or your balance runs out.
